📣 08/04/2026 v5.22.0

  • Removed total block count.

    • The extension previously tracked all blocked content to provide a total count of blocked content, but this caused performance issues and lag when blocking a large amount of content. I decided to remove it since the performance cost outweighed the benefits. Sorry to anyone who experienced lag because of this, and to those who enjoyed seeing the total count. The current session's block count in the popup remains.
